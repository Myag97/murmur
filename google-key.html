Getting your free Google key

About five minutes, once. The key lets Murmur use Google's best voices when you're online. The first million letters each month are free. Murmur stops at 300,000 letters on your computer, 300,000 in the phone's web reader and 300,000 in the Pixel app, so even all together you stay inside the free amount.

  1. Open Google Cloud and sign in with your Google account. Agree to the terms if it asks.
  2. Google asks for a card to confirm you're a real person. Click Start free or Activate at the top and follow the steps. You won't be charged for the free amount.
  3. Open the Text-to-Speech page and click Enable. If it asks you to pick or make a project first, make one called Murmur.
  4. Open Credentials. Click Create credentials, then API key. A box shows the key.
  5. Make the key work only for voices: click Edit API key (or the key's name). Under API restrictions, choose Restrict key, tick Cloud Text-to-Speech API, and click Save.
  6. Copy the key (the copy icon next to it).
  7. Safety alert (do this): open Budgets & alerts, click Create budget, name it Murmur, click Next twice, set the amount to 1 dollar, and click Finish. Google then emails you if anything is ever charged, even a cent.
  8. In Chrome, right-click the round purple M at the top right, choose Voice, speed and settings, open Google's voices (when online), paste the key and click Save. You'll hear the voice say hello.

When the free trial ends (after 90 days), Google will offer to upgrade or activate a full account. Don't click it. Without it nothing is ever charged; the key just stops working and Murmur uses its own voices. Ask Claude first if you want to keep Google's voices.

What gets sent: only the text being read aloud, to Google. Your dictation never leaves your computer.
