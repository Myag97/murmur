Murmur is installed

Murmur reads web pages, PDFs, Word files and Google Docs out loud in a natural voice. It runs on your own computer, so it's free with no limits.

1 Put the Murmur button where you can see it

At the top right of Chrome, click the puzzle piece, then click the pin next to Murmur Reader. A purple M button appears. One click on it reads the page you are on. Right-click it for the voice and settings.

🧩 puzzle piece→ Murmur Reader 📌→ M in your toolbar

2 Your voice

Your Mix is already chosen. Press Listen to compare the variants; Use this voice switches. You can change it any time.

Other languages

Murmur notices when a paragraph is in Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese or Hindi, pronounces it properly, and switches to the voice shown here. (Japanese and Chinese voices exist too, but need more work before they sound right.)

3 Getting the voice ready

The first time, Murmur downloads its voice (about 325 MB). After that it starts in a second or two.

4 Try it

How to use Murmur

Read a whole pageClick the M button. It starts reading; click again to pause.
Read just part of a pageSelect some text with your mouse or finger. A small ▶ button pops up next to it. Click it.
Jump aroundWhile it's reading, click any sentence and Murmur jumps there.
PDFs, Word files, Google DocsOpen the PDF or Google Doc in Chrome and click the M. For a file on your computer, right-click the M, then Open a PDF, Word file or text.
Save as audioIn the reader, press Save as audio to get an MP3 you can listen to anywhere.
Faster or slowerUse the speed menu on the little player bar, or right-click the M for the settings.

Keyboard shortcuts are optional: Alt+R reads or pauses.
